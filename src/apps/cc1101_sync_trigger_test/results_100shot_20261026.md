# CC1101 skew: 100-shot confirmation run (2026-10-26)

This run tested the six configs in `configs_to_test.md` with `host/skew_sweep.py --shortlist --port /dev/ttyUSB0`.
- **Shots:** 100 valid shots per config, 601 shots in about 15 min.
- **Data:** `readouts/skew_confirm_20261026.{csv,log}` and `_summary.txt` (local only, gitignored).

**Method:**
- **Skew** is CH2 − CH1 at the first falling edge of the two receivers' GDO2 outputs, in µs.
- **Receivers:** every config had them at a 160 kBaud data rate (MDMCFG4 low nibble 0xC, MDMCFG3 0x93).
- **Restore:** all nodes were put back to ASK, TX 1k, RX 160k, 325 kHz, AGC 05/00/91 afterwards.

## Findings
- **Only FSK, TX 50 kBaud, 541 kHz, AGC C3/00/91 kept every shot under 1 µs** (max 0.87 µs).
- **The 10-shot sweep was optimistic.** With 100 shots, every p90 rose by about 0.1–0.3 µs. For
  example, ASK at 541 kHz went from 0.35 to 0.67 µs.
- **FSK with AGC 03/40/91 had one whole-bit outlier** (19.9 µs = one bit at 50 kBaud). Its p90 is the
  lowest, but one late bit in 100 rules it out as a trigger.
- **541 kHz beats 203 kHz.** Every 203 kHz config had 3–5 shots over 1 µs.
- **ASK, TX 10k, 541 kHz is the runner-up:** one shot at 1.07 µs, no whole-bit outliers.
- **No receiver is systematically earlier:** every median is within ±0.2 µs.
- **Setup:** two ESP-NOW register-dump replies were lost and recovered automatically by re-broadcast.
  Every config was verified on both receivers.
